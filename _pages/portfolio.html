Research & Systems

From Human Mental Models to Agent Collaborative Competence

Effective collaboration depends on maintaining mental models of shared goals, partner intentions, and one's own reasoning. I study this along three complementary lines — capturing human mental models, simulating agent collaborative competence, and building open research infrastructure — unified in a single research hub.

ALMANAC

Humans' ALMANAC: A Human Collaboration Dataset of Action-Level Mental Model Annotations for Agent Collaboration

A dataset of humans' Action-Level Mental model ANnotations for Agent Collaboration, built from the Map Task. At every action, participants annotated their mental models: team goals, partner intentions, and their own reasoning.
